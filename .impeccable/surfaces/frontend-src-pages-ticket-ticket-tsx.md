## Scope

Replacement visual world for the whole CKD-Ticket counter app, superseding the Gym Whiteboard. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: seated counter staff and the owner on a 1366×768 laptop; the job is selling entry and closing the day. Action: choose a price, a slip prints. Constraints kept: Roboto + Nokora and the Khmer-tuned contrast; the amber family mark on the committing action; light-only. The owner chose the category standard, naming Square Dashboard as the craft bar, and the sibling till already runs that convention against Square POS.
