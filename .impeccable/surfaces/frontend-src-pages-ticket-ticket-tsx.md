## Scope

Replacement visual world for the whole CKD-Ticket counter app. Visitor mode: Operate. Audience: seated counter staff and the owner on a 1366×768 laptop; the job is selling entry and closing the day. Action: tap a price tile, a slip prints. Constraints kept: Roboto + Nokora typography and the Khmer-tuned contrast; the amber family mark on the committing action; light-only.
